About the Lab

Ideas. Built. Tested. Iterated.

VaporWare Labs is a growing collection of engineering projects, experiments, and shared knowledge.

Projects range from autonomous vehicles and smart-home systems to project-management software, local artificial intelligence, self-hosted infrastructure, and documented cooking experiments.

The lab is not limited to one technical field or product. It is a place to follow curiosity, build useful things, preserve what was learned, and make the results available to others.

Design Philosophy

Build small. Test honestly. Preserve what matters.

Documentation is treated as a first-class engineering artifact. Whenever practical, projects preserve not only what was built, but why important decisions were made.

  1. 01Start with a useful experiment.
  2. 02Build the smallest workable version.
  3. 03Test it in the real world.
  4. 04Study the results.
  5. 05Improve the design.
  6. 06Document what was learned.
  7. 07Share the results.
